Popis

WatsoNova is a new residential project in the center of Košice, which is being built on the site of the former Eastern Slovak Printing House - just a few minutes walk from the historical center of the city. The project will offer modern apartments with thoughtful layouts from 1-room to spacious 5-room units, underground parking, and up to 9,500 m² of commercial and business space. The architectural design is by the renowned Czech studio Kuba & Pilař architects, known for their emphasis on natural light, space, and sustainability. The project will also include relaxation zones, community gardens, playgrounds, shops, services, and restaurants - everything for comfortable urban living within reach. The first phase of the project will be completed in 2027. WatsoNova is the ideal choice for those looking for quality housing with a view to the future - in the heart of the city, but with an emphasis on privacy, community, and comfort.
